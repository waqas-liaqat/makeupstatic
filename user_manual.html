2. Core Operational Workflows

📊 2.1 Dual POS Report Upload (Daily Register Sales)

Use this tool to import daily sales reports exported from your store registers.

  1. Click Upload POS Files on the top toolbar. Select the branch (Beitar, Beit Shemesh, Jerusalem) and the transaction date.
  2. Upload File 1: Drop the item sales report Excel file (sales_item_lines_report_6004.xlsx). Automatically extracts SKUs, descriptions, quantities, and prices.
  3. Upload File 2: Drop the customer register summary file (sales_report_3250_6004.xlsx) to match customers by phone/name. (Click Skip File 2 for anonymous sales).
  4. Preview & Verification: Matched customers and products are displayed cleanly. Unmatched products/customers are flagged with warning notes.
  5. Click Import Transactions to deduct physical stock from the selected branch and write records to Airtable.

🖼️ 2.2 Invoice OCR (AI Receipt & Invoice Scanner)

Scan photo receipts or PDF invoices for both Customer Sales and New Inventory (Supplier Invoices).

Mode A: Customer Sale Receipt

Upload receipt image. AI extracts items, customer phone, and email. Deducts stock from selected branch.

Mode B: New Inventory / Supplier Invoice

Upload supplier invoice (e.g. INGLOT). Missing products are automatically created in Airtable catalogue, PO logged, and stock added.

🔄 2.3 Branch Stock Transfer

Move physical stock between store locations (e.g. 10 units from Jerusalem to Beitar).

  • Click Transfer on the top toolbar.
  • Select Source Branch (From) and Destination Branch (To).
  • Select Product and Quantity, then click Execute Transfer.
  • Stock is deducted from Source and added to Destination simultaneously in real-time.

2.4 Manual Single Transactions

Log individual manual sales, stock adjustments, or damaged inventory write-offs.

  • Click Manual Transaction button.
  • Select Type (Sale, Stock Received, Adjustment, Return).
  • Specify Product, Branch, Quantity, and Date.
  • Click Save Transaction.

2.5 Purchase Orders (POs)

Create and track supplier shipments.

  • Click New PO to log an upcoming order.
  • When shipment arrives at store, open Purchase Orders view.
  • Click Mark as Received to automatically add items into physical branch stock.

🛡️ 3. Data Integrity & Matching Rules

Customer Identification Rule:

Customers are matched via Phone Number → Email → Exact Name → Partial Name. If no match is found, the transaction is saved cleanly without a customer link. The dashboard never auto-creates fake customer records on sales.

Supplier Invoices vs Sales Invoices:
  • Supplier Invoices (New Stock): Missing products are automatically created in the master Airtable Products catalogue with SKU, Name, Supplier, and Cost Price.
  • Sales Invoices & POS Uploads: Missing products are logged as transactions with a warning note, while catalogue auto-creation is disabled to prevent accidental entry of corrupted sales SKUs.
Real-Time Stock Updates:

Branch stock fields (Beitar Stock, Beit Shemesh Stock, Jerusalem Stock) update instantly in Airtable upon transaction submission.

6. Universal Excel & CSV Export (Hebrew Support)

Instant Download

Every table and report in the system features instant Export Excel (.xlsx) and Export CSV buttons.

🇮🇱 Seamless Hebrew Character Display (UTF-8 BOM)

In standard systems, Hebrew product names often appear as garbled characters (???? or ×ž×™×§×...) when opened in Microsoft Excel. מיקאפ · MAKEUP BOUTIQUE automatically embeds the UTF-8 Byte Order Mark (\uFEFF) into all CSV exports and generates native multi-sheet Excel files. Hebrew text opens crisply and perfectly formatted on Windows, Mac, and mobile devices.

Exporting Options:
  • Export to Excel (.xlsx): Formatted spreadsheets via SheetJS.
  • Export to CSV: Lightweight raw text files with UTF-8 BOM.
  • Print / PDF: Clean printing layout for paper records.
What's Included in Exports:
  • Full SKU codes, Hebrew product names, brands, and shades.
  • Accurate branch breakdown quantities.
  • Actual prices paid after discounts and promotions.

8. Smart Israeli OCR Receipt & Invoice Scanner

Advanced AI Engine

The built-in receipt scanner recognizes Israeli cash register slips, supplier invoices, and delivery notes.

🏷️ Barcode & Catalog Priority

The scanner prioritizes catalog numbers (e.g. 100059) printed under item descriptions. If there is no barcode or SKU, it leaves it blank and matches via learned memory.

💰 Actual Price & Discounts

If a receipt has an overall discount (e.g. 10% / ₪61.40) or promotional ₪0 gift items, the system distributes the discount proportionally to save the actual price paid per item, ensuring the line totals match the receipt total (e.g. ₪552.60) exactly.

👩‍💼 Cashier vs Customer

Names appearing next to "מוכר/ת" (Seller/Cashier, e.g. רבקי רבינוביץ) are saved as the Employee / Salesperson, never misattributed as a Customer.

🧠 One-Click "Confirm & Remember" Machine Learning

If a receipt line is unrecognized or matched to the wrong item, simply click Change, select the correct catalogue product, and click Confirm & Remember.

Level 0: Saved Memory
Instant match from previously confirmed mappings.
Level 1: Exact SKU
Exact match on printed catalog number.
Level 2: Barcode Lookup
Scanned EAN/UPC barcode cross-reference.
Level 3: Keyword Match
Fuzzy Hebrew brand and product keyword search.

9. Negative Inventory & Stock Guardrails

Loss Prevention

Standard inventory apps silently clamp negative numbers to zero, hiding stock shrinkage and data discrepancies. מיקאפ · MAKEUP BOUTIQUE tracks true physical deficits (e.g. -3 units) so inventory anomalies can be audited and corrected.

Common Causes for Negative Stock:
  • Delayed Supplier Receipt: Items sold from the shelf before the supplier PO was received in the system.
  • Unrecorded Transfer: Products moved from Beit Shemesh to Beitar without logging the transfer.
  • Barcode Mismatch: Similar shade scanned under the wrong SKU at checkout.
How to Resolve Negative Stock:
  • Open Product 360° and review the Transaction ledger.
  • If a delivery was missing, receive the supplier PO.
  • If an error occurred, use Manual Sale → Inventory Correction (+/- units).

10. Data Rules, Airtable Sync & FAQs

Help & FAQ
❓ How often does the system sync with Airtable?
The system synchronizes in real time. Every sale, transfer, customer addition, or purchase order is written directly to Airtable via REST API. To pull latest external changes, click the Refresh button in the topbar or sidebar.
❓ Can multiple staff members use the dashboard simultaneously?
Yes! Because data is stored in the centralized Airtable cloud, multiple managers and cashiers can log sales, review reports, and transfer stock from different devices without database conflicts.
❓ Why did an invoice item show cost ₪0?
Some supplier invoices or sale receipts include complimentary gift items (מוצר מתנה). The scanner detects zero-cost items and calculates the adjusted average cost so that gross margin metrics remain 100% accurate.
❓ How do I add a brand-new product line to the catalogue?
Simply add the new row to your Airtable Products table with SKU, Product Name, Brand, Shade, Cost Price, Selling Price, and Min Stock thresholds. Refresh the dashboard, and the item is immediately active across all branches.

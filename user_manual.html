1. Dashboard Overview & Interface Layout

The makeupstatic Inventory Dashboard provides real-time stock control across all 3 retail branches (Beitar, Beit Shemesh, and Jerusalem), connected directly to your Airtable database. Hosted live at makeupstatic.vercel.app — works on any device or browser.

2. Core Operational Workflows

📊 2.1 Dual POS Report Upload (Daily Register Sales)

Import daily sales reports exported from your store registers. Primary method for batch-processing end-of-day sales.

1
Click Upload POS Files. Select Branch and Transaction Date.
2
File 1: Upload sales_item_lines_report_6004.xlsx. SKUs, quantities, and prices are extracted automatically.
3
File 2: Upload sales_report_3250_6004.xlsx to match customers. Click Skip File 2 for anonymous sales.
4
Review the preview. Matched customers show green tags. Unmatched products show yellow warnings.
5
Click Import Transactions — stock is deducted and all records are written to Airtable immediately.
💡 Tip: Run this every day after closing. Handles multiple items at once — much faster than manual entry.

🖼️ 2.2 Invoice OCR (AI Receipt & Invoice Scanner)

Take a photo of any paper receipt or invoice. GPT-4o Vision extracts all line items automatically. Works for both customer sale receipts and supplier delivery invoices.

🛒 Mode A: Customer Sale Receipt
  • Select Sale invoice type
  • Upload photo of the receipt
  • AI extracts items, customer name, phone, and email
  • Review and click Deduct from Stock & Save
  • Stock is reduced from the selected branch
📦 Mode B: Supplier Invoice (New Stock)
  • Select New Stock / Supplier type
  • Upload photo of delivery invoice (e.g. INGLOT)
  • AI extracts all SKUs, quantities, and cost prices
  • Review and click Create PO & Add to Stock
  • A Purchase Order is created and stock is added

Step-by-Step:

1
Click Invoice OCR. Select invoice type, branch, and date.
2
Drag & drop the invoice photo, or click to browse. Supported: JPG, PNG, HEIC.
3
Click Analyze with AI. The system compresses the image and sends it to GPT-4o Vision. Takes 5–15 seconds.
4
Review the extracted table. Each row shows SKU, Description, Qty, Price, and a Matched or Not found badge.
5
Edit any incorrect values in the table. Delete unwanted rows with the ✕ button.
6
Click the import button to commit to Airtable. All stock changes are applied immediately.
⚠️ "Not found": The product does not exist in your Airtable catalogue yet. For sales, the transaction saves with a note. For supplier invoices, add the product to Airtable or correct the SKU before importing.

🔄 2.3 Branch Stock Transfer

Move physical stock between store locations.

1
Click Transfer on the top toolbar.
2
Select Source Branch (From) and Destination Branch (To). They must be different.
3
Search and select the Product. Current stock levels per branch are shown.
4
Enter Quantity and Date, then click Execute Transfer.
5
Stock deducted from Source and added to Destination simultaneously. Transaction record saved in Airtable.

✏️ 2.4 Manual Transactions

For one-off entries: single sales, damaged goods write-offs, manual corrections.

1
Click Manual Transaction.
2
Select Type: Sale / Stock Received / Transfer / Manual Correction.
3
Search product, select branch, set quantity and date.
4
Optionally fill in customer info and a reference note.
5
Click Save Transaction.

📦 2.5 Purchase Orders (POs)

Track supplier shipments from order through to receiving into stock.

1
Click New PO. Fill in Supplier, Product, Qty, Cost, and Destination Branch.
2
Status moves: Draft → Ordered.
3
When shipment arrives, open the Purchase Orders view.
4
Click Mark as Received. Enter qty and date. Stock is added automatically. Status becomes Received.

3. OCR Smart Product Matching — How It Works

When the AI extracts a product from an invoice, it tries to find a match in your Airtable Products catalogue using 3 levels — from most precise to most flexible.

1
Exact SKU Match
Invoice SKU exactly matches a product SKU in Airtable (case-insensitive). Fastest and most accurate.
Example: Invoice SKU 5901905033 → Product SKU 5901905033 ✅
2
Partial SKU Match
If OCR reads a truncated barcode, the system checks if the invoice SKU contains or starts with a known product SKU (min. 6 digits). Handles photos that cut off part of a barcode.
Example: Invoice SKU 59019050334 → matches Product SKU 5901905033 ✅
3
Description Keyword Match
Last resort — significant words from the product name in your catalogue must all appear in the invoice description text.
Example: INGLOT ALL COVERED UNDER EYE → matches product All Covered Under Eye ✅
✕
Not Found
None of the 3 levels matched. The product does not exist in your Airtable Products catalogue. Add it manually in Airtable, or correct the SKU in the OCR row before importing.

🛡️ 4. Data Integrity & Business Rules

Customer Identification:

Matched via Phone → Email → Exact Name → Partial Name. If no match is found, the sale saves without a customer link. The system never auto-creates fake customer records.

Stock Minimum Floor:

Branch stock is never allowed to go below zero. If a sale deducts more than available, the stock floors at 0.

Supplier vs Sales Invoices:
  • Supplier Invoices: Add stock to destination branch and create a PO record.
  • Sales Invoices & POS Uploads: Deduct stock from selected branch. Missing products save with a note — no auto-creation to prevent corrupted entries.
Real-Time Airtable Sync:

Every action writes directly to Airtable in real-time. No batch sync required. The "Updated" timestamp (bottom-left) shows the last successful sync.

Minimum Stock Alerts:

If a branch's stock for a product falls below its minimum threshold (set per-product in Airtable), the product appears in the Low Stock banner on the Overview page.

5. Troubleshooting & FAQs

❌ Error loading: AT 401
The Airtable API token is missing or expired. Contact your admin to update the token in the app configuration.
❌ OCR error: Failed to fetch
Try: (1) Use a clearer photo. (2) Check your internet connection. (3) Hard-refresh the page (Ctrl+Shift+R) and try again.
❌ OCR error: OpenAI 401 — Incorrect API key
The OpenAI key has been revoked. A new key must be generated at platform.openai.com/api-keys and updated by your admin.
⚠️ Products showing "Not found" after OCR
The product does not exist in Airtable yet. Either: (a) add it to the Products table in Airtable and retry, or (b) manually correct the SKU in the OCR row to match an existing product before importing.
⚠️ Dashboard shows dashes (--) for all stock values
Data is still loading from Airtable. Wait 5–10 seconds. If dashes persist, click Refresh Data in the bottom-left sidebar. If the issue continues, check your internet connection.
❓ How do I add a new product to the catalogue?
Go to your Airtable base → Products table → add a new row with SKU, Name, Brand, Cost Price, Sale Price, and minimum stock thresholds. The dashboard picks it up automatically on next refresh.
❓ Can I use Invoice OCR on a PDF?
Yes. Take a screenshot of the PDF as a JPG or PNG and upload that image. The AI reads both printed and handwritten text in Hebrew and English.
❓ What if I import a POS file twice by mistake?
Each import creates new transaction records and deducts stock again. To fix a duplicate, use Manual Transaction (type: Manual Correction, positive quantity) to add back the excess deducted stock for the affected branch and product.
